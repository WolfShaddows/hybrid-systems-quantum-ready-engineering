# Initial research status

**Evidence date:** November 17, 2026. Sources are in [SOURCES.md](../references/SOURCES.md). Product capabilities change; verify current support before decisions.

IBM documents COBOL/Java interoperability on z/OS and JSON/API mapping to COBOL assets through z/OS Connect; this does not prove support in every COBOL environment. NIST published FIPS 203 (ML-KEM), 204 (ML-DSA), and 205 (SLH-DSA), but an algorithm standard does not define every protocol or ecosystem integration. Qiskit Aer simulates circuits classically and is not evidence of quantum advantage or PQC security.

Red Hat documentation reviewed on this date describes PQC features in specific RHEL releases and OpenSSL versions and distinguishes provider modes. Compatibility depends on exact release, protocol, format, and support status. OpenShift needs separate testing.

Open questions include the target COBOL compiler and ABI, Granite revisions/hardware, concrete PQC interoperability tests, exact RHEL/OpenShift releases, FIPS scope, and pinned Qiskit baseline. This is context, not a completed experiment.
